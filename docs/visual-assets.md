## Current theme image edits

Mode: built-in imagegen image editing, using each previous image as a visual reference. The seven edited images above were exported as optimized WebP files. The prompt set was:

- **Hero:** Preserve the black monolith, orbiting paths, icons, and composition; replace amber, bronze, and orange with brushed silver, white glow, cool gray, and deep charcoal. No text or logos.
- **Fitness:** Preserve the trainer, client, pose, gym, and framing; replace orange ambient glow with balanced daylight, charcoal, stone, and off-white. Keep natural skin tones.
- **Studio:** Preserve the booking interaction, tablet, layout, and framing; replace amber light and orange accents with soft daylight, charcoal, stone, and cream.
- **Clinic team:** Preserve the two people reviewing a laptop in the dental clinic; change the warm orange cast to neutral daylight and charcoal, stone, and off-white surroundings.
- **Café arrival:** Preserve the owner welcoming a couple, their phone, and the composition; use soft daylight, neutral surfaces, and muted plants while keeping people natural.
- **Dental patient:** Preserve the patient and clinician in the treatment room; change the warm orange and rust tones to daylight, stone, charcoal, and taupe.
- **Dental greeting:** Preserve the clinician and patient greeting at reception; replace amber and rust accents with daylight, charcoal, stone, white, and muted green.

### Hero signal sculpture

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium visual for Nexabizz marketing website hero. Primary request: an original three-dimensional sculpture representing a customer journey from digital signal to confirmed appointment, not a megaphone and not a copy of the supplied references. Subject: a glossy obsidian smartphone-sized monolith with a luminous circular amber aperture at its center, surrounded by two elegant flowing copper-orange light ribbons and a few subtle glass signal tiles. Style/medium: high-end physically rendered 3D product art, polished black enamel, smoked glass and brushed copper. Composition: centered hero object on a very dark espresso to near-black background, 4:5 portrait framing, generous negative space around object, dynamic three-quarter perspective. Lighting/mood: deep warm cinematic lighting, saturated burnt orange glow, amber highlights, soft reflections, premium editorial art direction. Color palette from references: #120807, #2B150D, #DB5317, #DA5F0E, #DAAE87. Constraints: no text, no logos, no recognizable app UI, no people, no white background, no watermark, visually clear at website hero size.
